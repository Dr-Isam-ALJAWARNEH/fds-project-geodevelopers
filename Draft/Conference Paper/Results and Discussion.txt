Word Count: 1,000–1,500 words
What to Include:
  1. Presentation of Results:
     - Clearly present your findings using text, tables, and figures.
     - Highlight key trends, patterns, or anomalies in the data.
     - Ensure that all visuals (e.g., graphs, charts) are labeled and referenced in the text.

  2. Interpretation of Results:
     - Explain the significance of the findings in relation to your research objectives.
     - Discuss how the results address the problem or question posed in the introduction.

  3. Comparison with Previous Studies:
     - Compare your findings with those from related literature.
     - Highlight similarities, differences, and any advancements your work offers.

  4. Implications:
     - Discuss the practical, theoretical, or methodological implications of your findings.
     - Explain how your results contribute to the field or solve real-world problems.

  5. Limitations:
     - Acknowledge any limitations in your study that may affect the interpretation of results.
     - Suggest areas for improvement or further research.

  6. Integration:
     - Seamlessly integrate the results and discussion to maintain a logical flow.
     - Avoid repeating the same information in different formats (e.g., text and tables).
